The experiments use Qwen3 checkpoints from [Qwen](https://huggingface.co/Qwen), model code from [Transformers](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers), and word readouts from [Neuronpedia's Jacobian lenses](https://huggingface.co/neuronpedia/jacobian-lens). They retain their upstream terms and are downloaded separately. The Jacobian-lens method is described in [Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html). Brief Qwen output examples in the notes are quotations of observed experimental behavior.

The release includes neither third-party model weights nor external corpora. See [agent instructions](AGENTS.md) for the separate automated-use workflow.
